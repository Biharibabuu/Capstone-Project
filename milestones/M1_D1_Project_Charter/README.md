## Brief
Establish the capstone business problem, objective, scope, team responsibilities, assumptions, risks, constraints and delivery plan.

## Adopted project framing
The current project target is **next-quarter EBITDA Margin (%) = EBITDA / Revenue from Operations × 100**. The revised D1 DOCX now identifies EBITDA Margin as the primary analytical output and carries that target into the company financial data plan. It positions the work as a company-level profitability forecast using public financial results and macroeconomic indicators, with scenario planning and comparison against a simple historical baseline.
